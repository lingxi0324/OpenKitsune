# OpenKitsune Trademark Policy

This policy explains how the OpenKitsune name, logos, icons, screenshots, and other brand assets may be used.

OpenKitsune source code is licensed separately under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (AGPL-3.0-only). That license does not grant trademark rights or permission to use OpenKitsune brand assets except as described here.

## Allowed Uses

You may use the OpenKitsune name in a factual way to:

- Refer to the upstream OpenKitsune project.
- Say that your project is "based on OpenKitsune" or "compatible with OpenKitsune".
- Link to the official project at `https://github.com/openkitsune/openkitsune` or `https://openkitsune.ai`.
- Preserve required copyright, license, NOTICE, and attribution information.

## Not Allowed

You may not, without prior written permission:

- Use the OpenKitsune name, logo, icon, or brand assets as the primary branding for a modified distribution, hosted service, product, company, domain name, app listing, package, container image, or social account.
- Present a fork, modified build, hosted service, or commercial offering as the official OpenKitsune project.
- Remove, obscure, or misrepresent upstream attribution, copyright notices, license notices, or NOTICE entries.
- Use OpenKitsune branding in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership, sponsorship, or official status.

## Forks and Modified Versions

Forks and modified distributions may use their own product names and branding, but they must:

- Preserve legally required OpenKitsune copyright, license, NOTICE, and attribution information.
- Clearly state that they are not the official OpenKitsune project if there is any chance of confusion.
- Avoid using OpenKitsune logos, icons, screenshots, or other brand assets as their own branding.

Examples of acceptable wording:

- "This project is based on OpenKitsune."
- "Compatible with OpenKitsune."
- "A fork of OpenKitsune with additional features."

Examples of unacceptable wording:

- "Official OpenKitsune Pro" for an unaffiliated product.
- "OpenKitsune Cloud" for an unaffiliated hosted service.
- Using the OpenKitsune logo as the app icon for a modified distribution.

## Permission

For permission to use OpenKitsune brand assets beyond this policy, contact the OpenKitsune maintainers.
